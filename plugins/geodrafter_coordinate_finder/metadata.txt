about=A coordinate finder and converter in one panel, with every format at once. Pick a position on the map (or let the panel follow the mouse) and read it in the map CRS, a CRS you choose, WGS84 decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, degrees-decimal-minutes, UTM (automatic zone including the Norway/Svalbard exceptions) and MGRS. Double click a row or press Ctrl+C to copy it, copy everything at once, or have a chosen format copied with every pick. The Go to field takes the same formats – also with N/S/E/W letters, decimal commas and UTM zone letters – centres the map there and marks the spot, so anything you copy can be pasted back. Points and vertices: get the coordinates of all (or the selected) points of a layer – written into its attribute table – or of every vertex of lines and polygons as a new point layer, or copy them as a table for a spreadsheet. For converting a list of coordinates, the panel links to the free GeoDrafter coordinate converter (geodrafter.com/coordinate-converter/). Built for QGIS 4 (Qt6), also runs on QGIS 3.34 and newer.

QGIS 3.34 and newer.
