supportsQt6=True
description=Capture map coordinates in any CRS plus WGS84 decimal degrees, DMS, DDM, UTM and MGRS – with copy buttons, copy-on-click and Go to (type a coordinate and jump there).
about=Click on the map (or track the mouse) to see the position in the map CRS, a CRS of your choice, WGS84 decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, degrees-decimal-minutes, UTM (automatic zone incl. the Norway/Svalbard exceptions) and MGRS. Every field has a copy button; "Copy on click" puts the format you choose on the clipboard with each click. "Go to" takes a typed coordinate in any of these formats (or two numbers in the chosen CRS), centres the map on it and marks it. Links to the GeoDrafter coordinate converter (geodrafter.com/coordinate-converter/). Based on "Coordinate Capture" by Stefanos Natsis (https://github.com/uclaros/qgis_coordinate_capture, GPL), a Python port of the former QGIS core plugin – thank you! Built for QGIS 4 (Qt6), also runs on QGIS 3.40.
