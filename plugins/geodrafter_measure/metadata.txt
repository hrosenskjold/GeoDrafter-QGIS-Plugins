description=Geodesic distance, area and radius measurement – keep measurements on the map and export them.
about=Measure distances (with segment lengths), areas (with perimeter) and radius circles on the ellipsoid, whatever the map projection – the same geodesic results as GeoDrafter's web tools (distance calculator, area calculator, radius map). Radius mode draws a true geodesic circle, either dragged with the mouse or from a typed radius such as "5 km" placed with one click. Metric, imperial or fixed units; results can be copied. Finished measurements can be kept on the map in "GeoDrafter distances" / "GeoDrafter areas" layers with their values as attributes or exported to a GeoPackage. Built for QGIS 4 (Qt6), also runs on QGIS 3.40.
