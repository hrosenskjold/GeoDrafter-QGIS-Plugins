about=A panel with every coordinate format at once. Pick a position on the map (or let the panel follow the mouse) and read it in the map CRS, a CRS you choose, WGS84 decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, degrees-decimal-minutes, UTM (automatic zone including the Norway/Svalbard exceptions) and MGRS. Double click a row or press Ctrl+C to copy it, copy everything at once, or have a chosen format copied with every pick. The Go to field takes the same formats – also with N/S/E/W letters, decimal commas and UTM zone letters – centres the map there and marks the spot, so anything you copy can be pasted back. For converting many coordinates at once, the panel links to the free GeoDrafter coordinate converter (geodrafter.com/coordinate-converter/). Built for QGIS 4 (Qt6), also runs on QGIS 3.40.
